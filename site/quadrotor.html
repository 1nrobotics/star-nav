Quadrotor · EGO-Planner · MuJoCo

A forest explored in flight

The quadrotor flies a 12-waypoint mission over a 9 × 9 m area among 150 pillars and tilted rings. EGO-Planner plans through the map it builds from a depth camera or a 360° lidar, and an SE(3) flight controller turns its setpoints into rotor thrusts.

Live in your browser

MuJoCo, compiled to WebAssembly, flies the quadrotor in this page in real time: the model, the forest and the flight controller of the simulator. No planner runs in the browser; the quadrotor follows the setpoints EGO-Planner sent in a recorded mission. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

Results

Each mission is an automated test that fails on any contact or waypoint timeout. In MuJoCo the planner's map is also compared with the simulator's obstacles.

Key facts

The planner sends position setpoints; the flight controller and the rotors are simulated, so the path flown is not the path planned.
