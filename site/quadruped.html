A house explored on four legs

The Lite3 explores a house and a forest on a waypoint mission, steered by CMU's local planner and walking with DeepRobotics' own learned policy. It goes under the table, around the furniture, up a ramp and down a flight of steps.

Live in your browser

MuJoCo, compiled to WebAssembly, simulates the Lite3 in this page in real time: DeepRobotics' model and walking policy, the house, and the joint control of the simulator. No planner runs in the browser; the robot follows the path CMU's planner led it along in a recorded mission. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

The interactive scene loads when you scroll here.

Blue: the path of the recorded mission. Orange: the path walked in your browser.

Drive gives the walking policy your command in place of the path: W and S walk, A and D turn, Q and E step sideways, or use the buttons on the scene. Nothing avoids the walls then.

Key facts

The planner is CMU's local planner for ground robots: its terrain analysis takes ramps and low steps as ground, and it sends the walking policy a velocity.
