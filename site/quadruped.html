The same navigation stack, on legs

DeepRobotics' Lite3 quadruped explores a MuJoCo house and forest with the planner the Star Nav drone uses. EGO-Planner plans at body height; a tracker turns its setpoints into velocity commands for Lite3's own learned walking policy; a terrain filter lets the planner take ramps and low steps instead of treating them as walls.

How it works

The planning stack is the drone's, unchanged except for its parameters. Two pieces are new: a terrain filter between the lidar and the planner's map, and a tracker between the planner's setpoints and the walking policy.

Control

Setpoint → joint torques

  1. Tracker. Velocity = setpoint velocity + 1.5 × position error, at most 0.8 m/s, rotated into the body frame. The robot walks in any direction, so it does not turn to face its path first. Turn rate = 1.5 × yaw error. It stands still within 3 cm and 3°.
  2. Walking policy. DeepRobotics' PPO policy from Lite3_rl_deploy, run unchanged. Its 45 inputs are body rates, gravity direction, the command, joint angles and velocities, and the last action. Its outputs are joint offsets from the standing pose.
  3. PD. The same gains as the upstream deployment, every physics step.
Planning

Lidar → walkable map

  1. The drone's map treats everything above the floor as an obstacle, ramps and steps included.
  2. The terrain filter keeps a 0.1 m grid of the lidar returns. Each cell records its lowest return, taken as the ground, and its highest return up to 0.55 m above that.
  3. Obstacle: more than 9 cm of height within the cell (walls, legs, boxes), or a ground 9 cm to 0.55 m above a neighbour's (box tops, platform edges).
  4. Free: ramps, low steps, and anything above the robot, so it walks under the table.
  5. Obstacle cells reach the planner as vertical columns, so it avoids them at any height.

Not yet
  • The pose is the simulator's ground truth; there is no odometry or SLAM in the loop yet.
  • The planner does not know the gait's limits: its 9 cm step threshold is above the 7 cm the policy climbs reliably.
  • The terrain filter is for static worlds: cells are never cleared.
  • EGO-Planner is a drone planner used here in one horizontal layer.
  • No hardware branch yet; CI does not run these tests because the model is downloaded.

Quick start

Ubuntu 20.04 with ROS Noetic and the workspace built as for the drone (see the drone page). The Lite3 model and policy are downloaded at pinned commits with a checksum, not committed.

01Install the walking robot

./src/scripts/setup_mujoco_env.sh --lite3   # onnxruntime + model + policy

02Watch it walk

roslaunch star_nav_bringup exploration_lite3.launch use_rviz:=true   # house, lidar
rostopic pub -1 /exploration/trigger geometry_msgs/PoseStamped "{}"

03Headless tests

rostest star_nav_bringup lite3_smoke.test                  # house, lidar
rostest star_nav_bringup lite3_smoke.test world:=forest    # pillar forest
rostest star_nav_bringup lite3_smoke.test world:=forest sensor:=depth
