DeepRobotics Lite3 · MuJoCo

The same navigation stack, on four legs

The Lite3 explores a house and a forest with the planner the drone uses, walking with DeepRobotics' own learned policy. It goes under the table, around the furniture, up a ramp and down a flight of steps.

Key facts

The planner is the drone's, unchanged except for its settings. New for legs: a tracker that turns setpoints into a walking speed, and a terrain filter that lets the planner take ramps and low steps.
