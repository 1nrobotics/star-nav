Live in your browser

MuJoCo, compiled to WebAssembly, simulates the T1 in this page in real time: Booster Robotics' model and walking policy, the house, and the joint control of the simulator. No planner runs in the browser; the robot follows the path CMU's planner led it along in a recorded mission. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

The interactive scene loads when you scroll here.

Blue: the path of the recorded mission. Orange: the path walked in your browser. When the T1 falls, as it sometimes does on the steps, the mission starts again.

Drive gives the walking policy your command in place of the path: W and S walk, A and D turn, Q and E step sideways, or use the buttons on the scene. Nothing avoids the walls then.

Results

Each mission is an automated test that fails on any contact, fall or waypoint timeout. The T1 completes the house mission in 8 of 10 runs; in the others it falls going down the steps.
